## Pre-Requisite Knowledge
- Probability - Machine Learning Systems, including transformer based models like ChatGPT, are probabilistic in nature and deal with uncertain (and therefore probabilistic, environments)
- Linear Algebra - Machine learning algorithms are implemented with matrices.
- Calculus - Derivatives and partial derivatives are a big part of optimisation algorithms which are a big part of analysing and training machine learning models.

There is a lot of maths in the first few weeks.
## Module Content
The Module Consists of:
- 25-30 Lectures
- 9 Lab Sessions
And requires a good amount of self study.

The assessment for the module is:
- A 2 hour MCQ exam worth 70%
- A practical assignment submitted at the end of the semester. All submissions are graded through an attack defence competition.
All grading is automated.
