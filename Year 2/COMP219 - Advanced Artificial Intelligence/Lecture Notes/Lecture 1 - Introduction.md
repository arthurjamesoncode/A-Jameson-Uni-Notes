## Perception-Cognition-Action Loop
AI agents universally have three steps:
- Perception - Recognising the state of the environment
- Cognition - Using the perception to compute something or draw conclusions. There is a focus on probabilistic inference.
- Action - Planning what to actually do, before acting on the environment

Perception can mean any things but there has specifically been a push for:
- Visual Recognition
- Voice Recognition

Cognition is primarily done through Probabilistic Inference. I.e. "Given what the model has observed what is the most likely explanation or conclusion within the given context".

Action refers to using the knowledge obtained by cognition to react to the environment.
