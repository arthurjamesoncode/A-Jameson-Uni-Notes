## Information Theory
Information theory is the mathematical theory of how we can express information efficiently. An important part of information theory is entropy, which is a mathematical model of the uncertainty of a specific variable. 

The technical definition of **information** is reduction of uncertainty. Our goal when learning a decision tree classifier is to efficiently decide which features we gain the most information from. As a result, we can use entropy to decide which features we can split on first.
