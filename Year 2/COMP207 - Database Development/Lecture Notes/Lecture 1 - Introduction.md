## What are Databases?
Databases are structured stores of information.

They are:
- Everywhere
- Valuable
- The drivers of the internet

To load a users homepage on Facebook, requires accessing hundreds of servers for all 2.09Billion daily users. As a result Facebook needs an efficient system for:
- Querying data
- Storing and manipulating data
And must ensure that it is handling all of it in a **safe** way.

Databases will always be needed even in the AI age. GenAI are intentionally lossy systems so they can never fully replace databases.
## Early DBMS and Relational Databases
Database Management Systems (DBMS) started off using an external data file which mapped values to objects or structs in programming languages.

These DMBSs were difficult to work with and not very robust especially when it came to dealing with many different updates in parallel. They were also very difficult to query efficiently or amend to add new fields.

Modern databases fare much better. They can manage huge amounts of data over a long period of time, and are highly flexible, efficient, and robust by comparison.

The Relational Model is the most common model for modern databases. Data is organised as relations, or tables. It can be queried at a high level by using Structured Query Language (SQL). This model was introduced in 1970 and became dominant by 1990.

They are one of the biggest success stories in all of computer science.
