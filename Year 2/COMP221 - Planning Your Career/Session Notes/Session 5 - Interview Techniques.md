## Demonstrating Questions
When asked open questions, you need to offer up various skills. The best way to illustrate these skills is with a STARR response. 

When offering skills it's best to provide examples that demonstrate the specific skills listed in the job interview. Because these questions are so open, they are actually the easiest ones to prepare for.

You can acquire and demonstrate skills in many different ways:
- Uni work
- Part time work
- Projects

It's also a good idea to come up with specific examples that you can use to show some specific skills. 

## STARR
STARR is a story telling technique that stands for:
- Situation - Where were you (university, job, etc.)
- Task - What did you have to do
- Action - What did you actually do
- Result - What happened
- Reflection - What did you learn

It is worth answering every question in a STARR format because it brings structure to your answers, and helps to avoid rambling answers.
