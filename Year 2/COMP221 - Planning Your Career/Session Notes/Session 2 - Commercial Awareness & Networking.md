## Commercial Awareness
Commercial awareness is an understanding of how industries and businesses work. It's an expected quality of everyone a business will want to hire.

Factors that feed into them are:
- Political Landscape
- Other organisations in the same sector
- Market Trends
- Tech advancements
- AI

**Places to look:**
- Company Website
- Social Media
- News/Publications
- Informational interviews
- People in the sector

Megatrends are powerful transformative forces that change the global economy. The four main ones are:
- Urbanisation
- Digitalisation
- Climate Change
- Social Expectation

Doing a SWOT analysis is a great way to understand a company you are interested in.

I'm now going to do one on Lloyds bank:

Strengths:
- Size & Established Name
- Largest market share for banking in the UK
Weaknesses: 
- Highly concentrated in the UK
Opportunities:
- Geographical expansion - Can tap into other markets in other places
- Gen AI - Can integrate generative AI to enhance their software services
Threats:
- Highly susceptible to UK market fluctuations

Interview questions:
- What do you know about our company?
- Why are you interested in this sector?
- What do you know about our business?
- Why have you applied for the role?
- Which of our values resonate with you?
- What are the challenges currently facing our sector?
