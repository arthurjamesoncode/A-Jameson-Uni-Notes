## Execution Tips
- If you need a second to think, say that you do and think for a second. Takes control of the situation rather than looking like your floundering.
- Try not to worry too much about how you did in the moment. We are not the best judges of success when it comes to interviews.
- Make sure you are not disturbed - Let people in your house know, turn off your phone etc.
- Dress professional, wear colours that stand out from your background
- Make sure lighting is fine
- Clean simple background
- Camera at the right height
- Smile and project warmth

## Preparation Tips
- Prepare a bank of evidence you can draw on for a range of skills
- Revisit the job description and do you research on the company
- DO NOT READ FROM A SCRIPT - Feels unnatural and is much worse than using some filler words or stumbling slightly
- Using post its/prompt sentences is good
- Learn about the organisations current work

## Types of Question
- Motivational/Values Based: "Why this role? Why this company?"
- Competency Based: "Tell me about a time when..."
- Strengths Based: "Tell me about a project you are proud of"
- Technical/Knowledge Based
- Hypothetical: "What would you do if"

## Motivation Based Questions
When answering evidence based question you can use the following structure:
- Motivation: The reason, tied to something specific about the organisation
- Evidence: Something you've done that demonstrates this motivation
- Fit: What you would bring and where you want to go
