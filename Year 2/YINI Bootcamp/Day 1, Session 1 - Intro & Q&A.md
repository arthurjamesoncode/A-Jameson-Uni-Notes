## Common Pitfalls in Application Process
People tend to fail out at 3 places:
- Sending CVs and cover letters
- Asynchronous Video Interviews
- F2F or Online Interviews

This is because:
- They don't tailor their CV and Cover Letter to each roll or haven't refined it to a high enough standard.
- They don't practice the asynchronous or final interviews enough

You can do mock interviews with the career studio. There is around an 80% conversion rate between mock interviews and passing the final interview according to chell.

You may have to apply for hundreds or one placement. It varies for everyone.

You cannot rely on employers to respond promptly.
## Using AI
You need to know how to use AI for pretty much all roles in this field. You should not be entirely reliant on it, you should be improving the output and adding a personal touch when using it for things such as your CV etc.

Be careful because:
- You need to sound like yourself when applying. If not they will realise your are not authentic during interviews.
- You have to be able to back up everything on your CV verbally in  case asked about it in an interview.
## Tailoring to a Role
CV:
- Place the most relevant experience at the top
- Taylor the personal statement to the industry or the role

Cover Letter:
- Look at the website of the company
- Subtly drop in the "values" of the company
- Learn about the work the company does/what problems they work on and write about that a bit

Another way of tailoring to the role is reaching out to the employer or someone at the company:
- Make sure the person you are contacting is the correct person. Look for hiring manager, or at least a management role.
- This works better for smaller companies
- If you have met someone from the company at a networking event reference this in the message
- Don't send a CV
- Find someone you know and ask them to refer you
- Be polite
